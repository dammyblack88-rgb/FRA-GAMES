# Social World, Creator Economy and Real-Life Connectivity

**Product:** NEXT WORLD (working product identity; final public title undecided)  
**Studio:** SimeonJr Studios  
**Repository:** `dammyblack88-rgb/FRA-GAMES`  
**Status:** Product requirements and future architecture direction only. None of the social network, livestreaming, real-device messaging integration, real-money payout or creator-fund capabilities described here should be considered implemented.

## 1. Core promise

NEXT WORLD should grow beyond a conventional game release into a living, continuously evolving 3D world and creator platform. SimeonJr Studios builds and maintains the foundation; players and creators help expand the world through approved content, communities, events and feedback.

The goal is to make the platform capable of evolving for many years, not to make an impossible promise that any online service can never shut down. Long-term survival depends on sustainable operations, security, backups, moderation, funding and a healthy community.

## 2. Two connected sources of evolution

### Studio-led evolution
- New regions, districts, careers, activities, missions, world systems and quality improvements.
- Performance, accessibility, security, moderation and anti-cheat improvements.
- Save compatibility, versioned content and safe migrations.
- Official events, competitions and creator programmes.

### Community-led evolution
- Players create original missions, stories, characters, businesses, homes, tournaments, videos and events.
- Guided forms, voice-assisted proposals and visual templates should make creation feel like playing—not like using professional development software.
- Drafts remain private until the creator chooses to submit or publish.
- Publishing uses validation, asset permissions, automated safety checks, human review where appropriate, reporting, takedowns, appeals and version rollback.
- Community feedback informs priorities but does not automatically grant content permission or publication.

## 3. Social layer

Planned social capabilities:
- Friends, profiles, presence and privacy settings.
- Direct messages, group conversations and community spaces.
- Text chat and optional voice chat.
- Parties, invitations, event calendars and community hubs.
- Mute, block, report, moderation and appeals.
- Audience controls and age-appropriate defaults.
- Cross-platform identity and communication only where technically and legally supported.

Communication must be safe by design. Users need control over who can contact them, who can see their activity, and whether they can be invited into a session. Private communications must not be exposed to other players or used for advertising.

## 4. Livestreaming and creator media

Players may eventually broadcast:
- Gameplay and roleplay.
- Original creator experiences.
- Football and other tournaments.
- Music, film, performance and community events.
- Tutorials, showcases and moderated competitions.

Required platform controls include:
- Stream start/stop, audience selection and privacy.
- Moderation tools, chat controls, blocking and reporting.
- Copyright and music rights handling.
- Age ratings and safeguards for live interaction.
- Streamer safety controls and delay options where needed.
- Storage/retention rules and consent for recordings.
- Abuse response and repeat-offender enforcement.

Livestreaming is a significant infrastructure and moderation cost. Start with controlled pilots and limited audiences before promising open global broadcasting.

## 5. In-game smartphone and real-device companion

The game should provide a virtual smartphone interface with two clearly separated environments.

### Fictional in-world phone
- NPC contacts and conversations.
- Fictional calls, messages and voice notes.
- Game businesses, mission alerts, maps, contacts and event invitations.
- Original fictional social applications and game services.
- Fictional balances and game-only financial services.

### Optional real-device companion
- Surface selected real-device notifications while playing.
- Explore supported quick actions for WhatsApp and other messaging services.
- Potentially support permitted reply actions, voice input, call controls and music playback controls.
- Let users configure notification allowlists, quiet hours, previews, mute and privacy settings.
- Never assume that every operating system or messaging service exposes the same functionality.

Real-device integration must rely on official provider APIs and documented operating-system capabilities, with explicit permission. Do not scrape WhatsApp, inspect private message databases, bypass encryption or platform security, impersonate the user, or promise sending/replying functionality until the exact supported flow has been tested and approved. Android and iOS capabilities may differ. The game must not upload private notification/message content to its servers by default.

## 6. Real creator economy

The platform should support legitimate real-world creator opportunities, separate from the fictional in-game economy.

Potential eligible activities:
- Creating original, approved game experiences and digital assets.
- Producing eligible videos and livestreams.
- Hosting tournaments, events and performances.
- Participating in funded sponsorships and official challenges.
- Selling approved digital creations if a compliant marketplace is later established.

Potential reward types:
- Cash payouts.
- Airtime and mobile data bundles.
- Sponsored prizes.
- Production equipment or support.
- Grants and creator-development funding.

### SimeonJr Creator Fund — proposal

SimeonJr Studios may establish a creator fund to invest in selected creators and community projects. Funding must come from an explicitly allocated studio budget, platform revenue or committed sponsorship. Selection criteria, budget, timelines and reward terms must be public and auditable. This is a proposed future programme, not a current fund or guarantee of income.

### Financial separation

- Virtual cash, properties, salaries, heist payouts and fictional investments are game-only.
- Real creator earnings are recorded in a separate real-world reward ledger.
- No fake withdrawable balances or guaranteed income claims.
- Fictional heist missions are entertainment and never a method of earning real cash, airtime or data.
- No pay-to-win or purchase of competitive dominance.

### Launch prerequisites

Before real payouts or a marketplace:
1. Legal review for Nigeria and each supported payout territory.
2. Payment provider selection and verified payout operations.
3. Tax, consumer protection, privacy and age compliance.
4. Eligibility, identity checks and clear terms.
5. Anti-fraud, bot, collusion and engagement manipulation detection.
6. Copyright, licences, attribution and dispute handling.
7. Auditable accounting, transaction IDs, approval trails and reconciliation.
8. Budget limits and a funded reserve; never promise unfunded rewards.

## 7. Platform trust and safety

Required before public social/creator launch:
- Age-appropriate defaults and restrictions for minors.
- Consent-based voice/video and recording indicators.
- Privacy-first messaging and notification handling.
- Reporting, blocking, moderation, appeals and takedowns.
- Copyright and licensed-asset checks.
- Anti-harassment, impersonation, scams and grooming safeguards.
- Livestream moderation and emergency escalation.
- Secure accounts, rate limits, anti-spam and fraud monitoring.
- Data minimisation, retention/deletion policies and incident response.

## 8. Staged delivery

1. **Foundation:** Document product contracts, permissions, privacy model, identity and moderation requirements. No public social launch.
2. **Private social prototype:** Friends, invitations and basic text chat with reporting/blocking and access controls.
3. **Creator tools pilot:** Private drafts, approved templates, limited mission/story publishing and rollback.
4. **Community events:** Controlled tournaments and events with moderation.
5. **Streaming pilot:** Limited creators/audiences, clear rights and operational controls.
6. **Real-device companion research:** Validate platform/provider APIs and permissions separately for Android and iOS. Build only supported integrations.
7. **Creator rewards pilot:** Funded competition or grant with published rules and real payment verification.
8. **Marketplace and scale:** Only after legal, fraud, moderation, infrastructure and financial operations are proven.

## 9. Non-negotiable implementation rules

- This document is a requirements record, not proof of implementation.
- No paid infrastructure, APIs, streaming services, cloud compute or subscriptions without explicit founder approval.
- No real-money rewards without an approved and funded budget.
- No WhatsApp or other messaging integration that bypasses official capabilities.
- Preserve the existing game repository, branch history, identity and world foundations.
- Keep the final public game title undecided until the founder chooses it.
